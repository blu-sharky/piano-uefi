Device tree blob for the Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro (piano, SM8750).

Source: extracted unmodified from the stock vendor_boot DTB area of the
author's own device, HyperOS OS3.0.308.0.WPYCNXM, variant vbdtb-04. The
stock ABL boot chain and the SM8750 closed-source DXE drivers expect the
vendor device tree, so an unmodified vendor blob is required for
interoperability.
